## Buys with the qty  [lines 139-145]

  141:     strategy.entry("DCA Buy", strategy.long, qty=positionSize)

## Take-profit close  [lines 159-165]

  161:     strategy.close("DCA Buy")

  163:     // Reset parameters after closing
