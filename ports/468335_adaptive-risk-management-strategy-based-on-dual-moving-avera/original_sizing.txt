## Risk 2.5 % of equity over a stop 1.5 % under the SMA 200 (size only)  [lines 125-139]
  125: // Set the stop-loss to 1.5% below the 200-day moving average
  126: stopLoss = ma200 * 0.985  // 1.5% below the 200-day MA
  127: 
  128: // Risk management (1.5% of total equity)
  129: riskPercent = 0.025  // 1.5% risk
  130: equity = strategy.equity
  131: riskAmount = equity * riskPercent
  132: 
  133: // Calculate the distance between the entry price (close) and the stop-loss
  134: stopDistance = close - stopLoss
  135: 
  136: // Calculate position size based on the risk amount and stop-loss distance
  137: if (goldenCross and stopDistance > 0)
  138:     positionSize = riskAmount / stopDistance
  139:     strategy.entry("Long", strategy.long, qty=positionSize)
