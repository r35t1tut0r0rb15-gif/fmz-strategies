## Equity-based order size and entry  [lines 231-232]
  231: ordersize=floor(strategy.equity/close) // Floor returns largest integer, strategy.equity gives total equity remaining - allows to dynamically calculate the order size as the account equity increases or decreases.
  232: strategy.entry("long",strategy.long,ordersize,when= buy) //strategy.entry let's you enter the market variables id ("long"), strategy.long (long position entry), size of the order and when the order should happen
