## How to read the "factor" column

- **Eurex R-factor**: the adjustment factor Eurex applied to its options and futures on the stock. It is multiplicative: pre-event prices × R = comparable post-event prices. For reverse splits, Eurex's R is 10 (pre-event prices are multiplied by 10).
- **Form 8937 allocation (spinoffs)**: the parent's share of combined value in the issuer's IRS Form 8937, meaning parent value / (parent value + spun-off value per parent share), at the valuation prices the issuer chose. Because it is a share of value, it can serve as the parent's price factor. The issuer's valuation date and price type (close, open, VWAP, open/close average) differ between companies; see "For the project chat".

**Note A (ex-date not printed as a single date).** For IBM, GE HealthCare and AT&T the documents give the mechanism rather than a printed "ex-date". The parent traded "regular-way" with the entitlement through the Distribution Date, and the spun-off stock began regular-way trading the next trading day. The ex-date shown is that next trading day, which the issuer documents name as the first trading day after the distribution (IBM 8937: "the date on which Kyndryl Holdings stock first traded"; AT&T 8937: "April 11, 2022, the first trading day following the Distribution"; GE HealthCare: distribution "after the close of trading" on 2023-01-03 with valuation on 2023-01-04). No exchange notice with a printed ex-date was retrieved for these three.

**Note C (Iberdrola 2019 and January 2020 ex-dates).** These three BORME (official gazette) notices define the last trading date as "el día de publicación de este anuncio" and open rights trading on the next calendar day. The ex-date shown is the day after the BORME publication date, which is taken from the notice's PDF path (e.g. /borme/dias/2019/01/08/). From July 2020 on, Iberdrola's CNMV notices print the ex-date directly.

**Note B (Alibaba ADS ex-date).** The 6-Ks give a common record date for ordinary shares and ADSs (HK and NY time respectively), but no NYSE ex-date for the ADS. The HKEX ex-dates are printed in the HKEX forms.
